    The same data run through three ported methodologies: Boris Chen (Gaussian-mixture tiers over expert-consensus ranks), elboberto (VBD vs. seat-count baselines), and the CSG sheet (VBD vs. games-played baselines, scarcity-adjusted) — at either horizon: this week (start/sit) or rest of season (trades & waivers). The common unit is projected points and points above replacement (VBD), comparable across players and positions. Both VBD bars share one axis; heavy rules mark Boris tier breaks.
